If Only I Could Be There With You… 🥹❤️

If only I could be there with you today… sitting beside you, looking at your smile and watching your eyes light up while you celebrate your special day.

I wish I could see your happiness with my own eyes, hold your hand, give you the tightest hug, and simply be there beside you.

But this is the hardest part of loving someone from a long distance… sometimes you have so much love to give, but you can't be there to give it in person. 🥹

I may not have everything I wish I could have with you right now. I don't have your hand in mine, I can't sit beside you, I can't surprise you by showing up at your door, and I can't see your beautiful smile when you read this.

But you know what I do have?

I HAVE YOU IN MY HEART. ❤️

No matter how many miles are between us, you are still one of the closest people to my heart. Distance can keep me away from your arms, but it can never keep my love away from you.

Sometimes I wish I could pause everything, cross all these miles, and just appear beside you for a little while… just to look at you and say,

“Happy Birthday, my love.” 🥹❤️

Maybe today I can't celebrate your birthday the way I want to. Maybe I can't be there to see your happiness, hear your laughter, or hold you close.

But InshaAllah, one day…

There will be no screens between us.
No miles.
No goodbyes.
No wishing that I could be there.

I'll just be there. With you. ❤️

And when that day comes, I want to celebrate every birthday with you, make you smile, annoy you, love you, and remind you again and again how much you mean to me.

Until then, whenever you miss me, look at this little infinity…

♾️

Because that's what I want my love for you to be.

Not temporary.
Not for a moment.
Not until distance gets difficult.

But forever. ♾️

I love you more than these words could ever explain.
